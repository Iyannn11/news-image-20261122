One image summing up Sunday 22 November 2026's world news: **35 verified
stories from 7 regions**, in a Top-1-style broadsheet — masthead, 6-fact
strip, 3 big cards, 2 rows of small cards, a "WHERE IT HAPPENED" map strip,
a "STORIES BY REGION" tally, a red DATE CHECK box, and an "ALSO ON SUNDAY ·
22 MORE STORIES" list.

- Big 3: COP31 overtime in Antalya · Verstappen wins the Las Vegas Grand
  Prix · Russia fires 550 munitions at Ukraine overnight.
- Every story happened, or was first reported, on Sunday 22 November 2026.
  Two scheduled Sunday events are framed as such (Pope's noon Angelus,
  SpaceX NROL-136 due 12:16 UTC). See `SOURCES.md` for every story's
  date and source, plus ~70 items checked and rejected as not-22-Nov.
- The entry image is `entry.png` (1024×1024), rendered deterministically
  by `render.py` (Python + Pillow, no network, no randomness).
- `main.ts` (Node 22) copies `entry.png` to the output directory.
